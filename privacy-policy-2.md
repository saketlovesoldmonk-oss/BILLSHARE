# Privacy Policy for BillShare

**Last Updated:** August 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction
BillShare ("we," "our," "us," or the "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Participant Information:** Name, phone number (optional)
- **Team/Group Data:** Team codes, participant lists, expense records
- **Device Information:** Device tokens for push notifications
- **Authentication Data:** Team codes and optional registration credentials

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information:** Device type, OS version, app version
- **Usage Data:** Features used, interaction patterns, app performance
- **Push Notification Tokens:** For sending notifications

### 2.3 Cloud Synchronization
When you enable cloud sync via Firebase, your expense data is transmitted to Firebase servers, including:
- Team codes
- Participant information
- Expense records and calculations
- Device tokens

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- **Core Functionality:** Split bills, calculate balances, manage groups
- **Notifications:** Send push notifications about expenses and settlements
- **Cloud Sync:** Store and sync data across your devices
- **Improvements:** Analyze usage patterns to improve the App
- **Support:** Respond to user inquiries and troubleshoot issues
- **Security:** Detect fraud and protect against abuse

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage:** Expense data is stored locally on your device's browser/app storage
- **Cloud Storage:** When cloud sync is enabled, data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database
- **Encryption:** All data transmitted to Firebase uses HTTPS encryption
- **Retention:** Data is retained as long as you use the App or until manual deletion

## 5. Sharing of Information

We **do not sell or share** your personal information with third parties, except:
- **Firebase:** For cloud synchronization (see their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
- **Push Notifications:** Firebase Messaging services
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or court order

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data
- **Delete** your data (tap "Reset App" to clear all local data)
- **Opt-out** of cloud synchronization (use the App in offline mode)
- **Opt-out** of push notifications (disable in app settings)

To delete your cloud data, contact: support@billshareapp.com

## 7. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications about:
- New expenses added to your group
- Settlement reminders
- Group updates

**You can disable notifications** in your device settings or within the App.

## 8. Third-Party Services

This App integrates with:
- **Firebase (Google):** Database, Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Hosting
  - [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Capacitor:** For native Android integration
  - [Capacitor Privacy](https://capacitorjs.com/)

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware of such information, we will delete it immediately.

## 10. Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Retained until you manually delete via "Reset App"
- **Cloud Data:** Retained while your team exists; deleted 90 days after team inactivity
- **Logs:** Server logs retained for 30 days for security purposes

## 11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted in the App with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of changes.

## 12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or concerns, contact:
- **Email:** support@billshareapp.com
- **Website:** www.billshareapp.com

## 13. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, we will:
1. Notify affected users within 72 hours
2. Provide details of the breach and remediation steps
3. Offer identity protection services if applicable

## 14. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in the United States and other countries where Firebase operates. By using the App, you consent to such transfers.

## 15. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether it is sold or disclosed
- Delete personal information
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information

The App does not sell personal information. Contact us for requests.

## 16. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

EU users have the right to:
- **Access** their data
- **Rectification** of inaccurate data
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict processing**
- **Data portability**

Contact our Data Protection Officer: dpo@billshareapp.com
